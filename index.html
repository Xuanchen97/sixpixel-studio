FAQ

Good questions.

Still unsure? Send a message — you'll get a straight answer.

Do I need a brand-new website?

Often not. Six Pixel starts with a free site check. If your current site can be fixed — speed, menu, hours, structured data — we fix it. We only rebuild when that is cheaper or clearly better for you.

What is GEO, and why does a restaurant need it?

GEO (generative engine optimization) means making your business easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews to understand and recommend. It relies on clear, consistent facts: a text menu, hours, location, cuisine, price range and structured data on your site and Google Business Profile.

How are the promo videos made?

Short vertical videos for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, built from your own photos and clips plus motion graphics and AI-assisted editing, captions and voice-over. Every video is reviewed by a person before you see it, and nothing is posted without your approval.

Can I update my menu and hours myself?

Yes. Sites are built so menus, prices and hours can be edited without a developer, or Six Pixel can handle updates on a monthly care plan.

Which areas does Six Pixel serve?

Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. Most work happens remotely, with an in-person visit for photos and video when needed.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what your business already has. After the free site check you get a fixed quote, with no ongoing commitment required.

Our story

Twenty-eight seats, one long table.

Saltwick started as a supper club in a shared kitchen. Now it's a small room on Dundas West where the menu follows the farms, the wine list follows small producers, and dinner never feels rushed.

Every dish on this page is written as real text — so guests, Google and AI assistants all see the same menu you do.
